Billiards

8-ball for two players on one device. Sink your group (solids or stripes, whichever you sink first), then the 8. Sink the 8 early and you lose.

Chess Pool against the computer or a friend on the same device. Shoot the white ball to knock the other side's pieces into the pockets. Their King goes last, like the 8-ball: once all their other pieces are sunk, sink the King on a later shot to win. Sink it early (or sink your own King) and you lose.

Sink one of yours and you shoot again. A foul (sinking the white, hitting nothing, or hitting the wrong group first) gives the other player ball in hand: they tap anywhere to place the white ball.

Press behind the white ball, pull back away from where you want it to go, and let go. The yellow line shows where the piece you'll hit goes.
Sink an enemy piece (and none of your own) to shoot again. If the white ball goes in, the other player has ball in hand: they tap anywhere to place it. Check: if the white ball stops within one square of your King, your King glows red and you may drag it anywhere open on your half before you shoot.
Pieces handle differently: pawns are light and scatter, knights spin when hit off-center and curve, rooks are heavy, the queen is lively, and the King is the heaviest.
