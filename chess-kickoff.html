Chess + Kickoff

Each turn, make a chess move, then flick one of your pieces at the ball. Your clock stops when you flick and the other player's starts once the ball stops. Every goal adds a minute to the scorer's clock. Win by checkmate, by scoring 3 goals, or on time. A drawn board goes to whoever has more goals.

Chess: click a piece then a highlighted square, or drag it.
Kickoff: press on one of your pieces, pull back away from where you want it to go, and let go. White shoots right, Black shoots left. A kickoff that goes into a goal doesn't count: it's a foul, and the pieces go back to the start for the other side's kickoff.
