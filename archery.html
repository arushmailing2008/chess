Pawn Storm Archery

Black's pieces are storming your back rank. Hold to draw your rook's bow and let go to shoot. A full draw (the ring turns gold) flies through every piece in its path. Don't let anything reach rank 1!

Mouse or finger: aim, hold, release. Keyboard: aim with the mouse, hold Space to draw, 1–4 to buy. P pauses.
Hits in a row build a streak: ×2 points from 5, ×3 from 10, ×4 from 20. A miss or a breach resets it.
After each shot the bow reloads (the white bar under the rook); a draw only starts charging once it's ready.
Pawns step straight down; knights (from 12 points) jump in L-shapes. Pawns are worth 1, knights 3, and each extra piece one arrow pierces is worth more.
Every 10 points is a new level, and the storm gets faster. From level 4 the board shakes, from level 5 the squares change color, from level 6 wind pushes your arrows sideways (a full draw drifts less), from level 7 the board flashes, and from level 8 the lights go out now and then, except around your rook and arrows.
