Chess Kickoff

Two players, one device. Take turns flicking one of your pieces at the ball. White shoots right, Black shoots left. First to 3 goals wins.

White 0 Black 0

Press on one of your pieces, pull back away from where you want it to go, and let go. The farther you pull, the harder the flick. The queen hits hardest; the king is the weakest.
A kickoff can't score: the goals stay shut until the first shot after a kickoff. Your turn ends when everything stops moving.
