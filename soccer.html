Chess Kickoff

Two players, one device. Take turns flicking one of your pieces at the ball. White shoots right, Black shoots left. First to 3 goals wins.

Each move: 10 seconds, or the turn passes

White 0 Black 0

Turn your phone sideways for a bigger field.

Press on one of your pieces, pull back away from where you want it to go, and let go. The farther you pull, the harder the flick, and the longer the arrow. The queen hits hardest; the king is the weakest.
A kickoff that goes into a goal (either one) doesn't count: it's a foul that costs the kicking side 30 seconds (or the match, with 30 seconds or less left), and the other side kicks off. Your turn ends when everything stops moving.
You have 10 seconds for each move (the countdown is at the top of the field), or the turn passes. With a clock, your time only runs while it's your turn to aim; each flick adds the increment, and running out loses.
